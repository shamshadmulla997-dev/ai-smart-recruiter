Our Project

What we built and why

AI Smart Recruiter & Career Guide is a full-stack web application that revolutionizes how candidates and recruiters connect. Powered by Java Spring Boot and MySQL, our platform uses intelligent skill-matching algorithms to analyze resumes, score job fit, and guide candidates on their career growth journey.

Users can upload PDF resumes, get instant analysis of their skills, discover their best-fit job roles, and receive personalized recommendations with curated YouTube learning links for missing skills. Recruiters can match candidates against any job role and see ranked results instantly.
